Getting Started on Android

  1. Open the Smart Sheet Android app and tap Sign in with Google.
  2. Enter an Employee Invitation Code (if invited by an Admin) or sign in directly to create your Admin workspace.
  3. In the Sheets tab, authorize Google Drive & Sheets access to connect or create spreadsheets.
