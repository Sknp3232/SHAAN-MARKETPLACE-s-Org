Smart Sheet Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your Smart Sheet (com.aistudio.smartsheet.kxmpzq) account directly inside the Android app or request deletion by email.

Option 1: Delete Immediately Inside the Smart Sheet App

  1. Open Smart Sheet and sign in with your Google Account.
  2. Go to My Account (for Employees, tap Account in the navigation bar; for Admins, open Admin → My Account & Legal Links).
  3. Scroll to ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm permanent deletion.

Option 2: Request Deletion Without the App

If you no longer have access to the Android app, email sanudeen42@gmail.com from the Google email address associated with your Smart Sheet account with the subject Smart Sheet Account Deletion Request. Requests are processed promptly and confirmed via email.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted: Your Firebase Authentication user account, your Firestore user profile, saved searches, employee workspace membership record (or Admin workspace settings/connections if you are a Workspace Admin), active Google OAuth token, and local device cache.
  • Not Deleted (Your Ownership): Original Google Spreadsheets stored in your personal or business Google Drive remain completely untouched in Google Drive.
  • Audit Trail Note: Immutable workspace audit/activity logs created prior to deletion may remain inside a team Admin's workspace history if applicable until the Admin deletes their workspace.
