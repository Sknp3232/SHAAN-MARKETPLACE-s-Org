This Privacy Policy describes truthfully how Smart Sheet collects, accesses, processes, stores, and deletes information based on the actual functionality of the Smart Sheet Android application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Sign-In & Account Profile Data: When you sign in using Google via Android Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication, Smart Sheet receives your Firebase user ID, display name, email address, and profile photo URL to identify your account and workspace role (Workspace Admin or invited Employee).
  • Workspace, Permission & Audit Metadata: Smart Sheet stores workspace configurations in Google Cloud Firestore, including connected spreadsheet IDs, custom sheet names, worksheet metadata, employee invitation codes, per-sheet employee permissions, saved searches, sync status logs, record addition/edit audits, and workspace activity logs.
  • Spreadsheet Content (Cached on Your Device): When you connect and sync a Google Sheet, worksheet headers and row values are cached locally in an on-device Room SQLite database so you can search records rapidly offline or on slow connections.
  • Device Preferences: Local DataStore preferences store your theme choice (System / Light / Dark) and any pending employee invitation code entered prior to sign-in.
  • Camera (Optional On-Device Barcode Scanning): If you tap the IMEI/barcode scanner, Android camera frames are processed strictly on-device via Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning to read barcode text. Smart Sheet does not record, store, or upload camera images or video.

2. Google Account Authentication

Authentication is handled exclusively through Google Sign-In (Jetpack Credential Manager) and Firebase Authentication. Smart Sheet does not collect or store your Google Account password.

3. Google Sheets Access & Requested Permissions

Each user authorizes their own Google Account via Google OAuth 2.0 (Identity.getAuthorizationClient) to connect Google Drive and Google Sheets. Smart Sheet requests only these two narrowest permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — Allows Smart Sheet to list, pick, and create only the specific Google Spreadsheets you open or create inside Smart Sheet. Smart Sheet does not request broad access to all files in your Google Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets — Allows Smart Sheet to read spreadsheet metadata/rows and append, update, or delete authorized rows in connected spreadsheets.

4. How Spreadsheet Information Is Accessed and Processed

  • Smart Sheet connects directly from your Android device to official Google Drive v3 and Google Sheets v4 APIs using your user-scoped OAuth access token.
  • Spreadsheet rows are indexed locally on your device for instant multi-keyword search, duplicate IMEI checking, and form field entry.
  • Fields identified as Aadhaar numbers are masked in search lists by default (XXXX-XXXX-last4) unless explicitly revealed by an authorized user or configured otherwise by the Workspace Admin.
  • Google Sheets in your Google Drive remains the authoritative source of truth for your records.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • All network traffic to Google Sign-In, Firebase, Google Drive, and Google Sheets APIs uses HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.
  • Cloud Firestore security rules enforce strict workspace and role isolation so unauthenticated users cannot read or write workspace data and employees only access assigned sheets.
  • OAuth access tokens are kept in memory for your active session, are never shared across different users on the device, and are redacted from error messages and diagnostics logs.

6. Sharing with Third Parties & Google Limited Use Compliance

Smart Sheet does not sell your personal data, share your spreadsheets with advertisers or data brokers, or use Google Workspace data to train AI or machine learning models. Data is processed only by Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure strictly to provide authentication, workspace database persistence, and Google Drive/Sheets synchronization.

Smart Sheet's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

  • Google OAuth Revocation: Tap Disconnect Google in the Sheets tab at any time to revoke the active OAuth token and clear local Google authorization state. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • In-App Account Deletion: Open My Account → Account Management → Delete Account to delete your Firebase Authentication account, remove your Firestore user profile and saved searches, revoke your Google OAuth session, and clear local cached rows. Your underlying Google Sheets files in Google Drive are never deleted when you delete your Smart Sheet account.
  • Web / Email Deletion Request: See our Account Deletion page or email sanudeen42@gmail.com from your registered email address.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to access your account data, request correction of profile or workspace records, disconnect external Google permissions, and delete your Smart Sheet account and associated app-stored data.
